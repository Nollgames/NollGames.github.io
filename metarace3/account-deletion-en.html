CRASH BAT RACING / LEGAL & SUPPORT

Account and Data Deletion

NollPang · Prepared November 5, 2026

Request deletion of your Crash Bat Racing account and data through the Game’s Settings or email, including when you cannot access the app.

1. Request deletion of a Crash Bat Racing account

You may request deletion of your Crash Bat Racing (the “Game”) account and associated game data, operated by NollPang. Email requests require neither installation nor a new purchase. This request does not delete accounts in NollPang’s other games.

Request deletion by email

If your email app does not open, write directly to admin@nollgames.com with the subject “Crash Bat Racing account and data deletion request”.

2. Request inside the app

  1. Sign in to the Game with the account you want to delete.
  2. Open Settings, check the account ID (UID) and copy it with the copy button.
  3. Select Delete Account.
  4. Confirm deletion in the dialog that says “Deleting your account will permanently destroy all your data.”
  5. If there is an error or the result is unclear, contact support with the copied UID and approximate time.

You can email us even if the app is unavailable or you cannot sign in. If you do not know the UID, provide minimum information you remember, such as nickname, login method (guest, Google Play Games or Apple Game Center), device and approximate last use. Do not send passwords, verification codes, full identity-document copies or full payment card numbers.

3. Verification and processing

For email requests, support verifies ownership and scope, processes the request without undue delay within applicable deadlines, and replies with the result. We request only minimum additional information if needed. If some data must legally be retained, we explain what, why and for how long.

After an in-app withdrawal request, Unity Gaming Services account and data deletion cycles may apply. Successful submission does not mean every operational, backup and analytics record is erased at that instant. Use the same address to request deletion of analytics or advertising information, or only specific personal data, and describe the scope.

4. Data deleted and retention exceptions

  • Account authentication information (Player ID and login links) and profile information such as nickname and country.
  • Race and boss-battle records and rankings, cars, drivers, pets, upgrades and power-up loadouts.
  • Account-linked game data such as gems and other currency, items, car pieces, reward records and purchase fulfillment records.
  • Deletion or anonymization of analytics and advertising data within the verified request scope.

Records required under Korean law may be retained separately with restricted purposes: contracts, withdrawal, payments and supply for five years; consumer complaints and disputes for three years; display and advertising records for six months. Provider operational and backup cycles may require additional time. See the Privacy Policy for details.

5. Before deleting

Once deletion is confirmed, progress and inventory are permanently erased and cannot be restored. Check the account before confirming. Include any purchase or refund issue in your request. Deletion is not an automatic refund request, but it does not waive statutory refund rights.

Uninstalling, logging out or unlinking Google Play Games or Apple Game Center is different from deleting the game-server account. This process concerns only your account in this Game, not your Google or Apple account or accounts in NollPang’s other games.
