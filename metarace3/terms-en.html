4. Races, upgrades, rankings and rewards

The Game is a car battle racing game in which you hit rival cars with a bat. Features include driver (character) melee skills, power-up items picked up during races and power-up deck building, pets that attack during races (upgraded with sandwiches earned in races, including pet trials), car and driver upgrades, boss battles that use boss tickets, and unlocking cars with car pieces collected from boss battles. Unlock requirements, costs and rewards are shown in the Game.

Content may be awarded through reward boxes earned by finishing in the top two or clearing a boss (with a waiting time before opening), daily quests, the 7-day gift, timed gifts, the special shop and advertising participation. Eligibility, limits, waiting times and reward destinations follow the relevant screen. We investigate missing or duplicate grants and make appropriate corrections based on the facts.

“Guess 1st place” is a mini game in which you predict the winner of a race. It is played only with in-game currency or by watching ads. Mini-game results and in-game currency and rewards cannot be exchanged for cash or prizes.

Rankings (leaderboards) and race opponent information may show other players your nickname, the country (flag) you selected in the Game, your selected car, driver and pet and their levels, your power-up loadout, and your score and rank. We may review suspected manipulation and correct confirmed invalid records. You may appeal through support.
