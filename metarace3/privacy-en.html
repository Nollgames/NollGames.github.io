3. Legal basis and public information

Authentication, game saving, rankings, fulfillment and support are processed as necessary to perform the service contract; statutory transaction records are retained to meet legal duties. Security and troubleshooting use an appropriate legal basis and necessary scope. Firebase analytics and Crashlytics error reporting are processed from the first app launch to the extent necessary to check errors and stability and improve the service; you may request suspension at any time under section 7. Unity Analytics collects data after you accept the Terms on first launch. Advertising, analytics and transfers requiring separate consent follow that procedure. Publication of this policy or acceptance of the Terms does not replace required separate consent.

Other players can see public profile information in rankings (leaderboards) and race opponent information, including your nickname, the country (flag) you selected in the Game, your selected car, driver and pet and their levels, your power-up loadout, and your score and rank. Ranking data delivered by the server also includes your game account identifier (Player ID). Email addresses, authentication tokens and purchase receipts are not shown in rankings. Avoid using your real name or contact details in a public nickname.

We do not disclose data beyond the described processing without a legal basis. Where separate disclosure requires consent, we identify the recipient, purpose, data and retention. Lawful court or authority requests are handled only within the law.

7. Advertising, analytics and choices

Advertising uses AdMob consent and privacy flows (Google UMP) and device settings. Where legally required, such as in the European Economic Area (EEA) and the United Kingdom, an advertising consent form is shown, and on iOS the App Tracking Transparency (ATT) permission request is shown. To change your choices, use the device settings below or contact admin@nollgames.com. Restricting personalized ads or denying tracking does not eliminate all processing for delivery, measurement and security.

Use Android’s available advertising-ID reset or deletion controls or iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. Menu names and options depend on OS and release version.

Firebase analytics and Crashlytics error reporting operate from the first app launch, and Unity Analytics operates after you accept the Terms on first launch. To request suspension or withdrawal concerning Firebase or Unity analytics, or deletion of existing analytics or advertising information, contact admin@nollgames.com. We verify the account and scope and explain the result and any lawful restriction. Terms acceptance alone does not justify processing requiring separate consent.

The app stores language and sound settings, login method and session token, Terms consent status, and tutorial and progress caches on the device. Clearing storage can affect preferences and guest access; it is different from deleting the server account. The app schedules on-device notifications for when reward boxes or timed gifts are ready (not server push notifications); you can turn them off in your device’s notification settings. These policy pages contain no Company-added advertising or analytics scripts or tracking cookies. GitHub separately handles website requests.

8. Access, correction, deletion and objection

You may request access, correction, deletion, suspension and withdrawal of consent, including through an authorized representative, at admin@nollgames.com. After minimum necessary verification, we respond without undue delay within applicable deadlines or explain the legal basis and scope of a restriction.

Use Settings > Delete Account to request withdrawal of your game account after confirmation. If the app is inaccessible, use the email path on the account and data deletion page. We confirm the scope of deletion of server accounts, progress, rankings and inventory, and additional analytics or advertising provider requests. Statutory transaction and dispute records are retained separately under section 5.

Uninstalling, logging out or unlinking Google or Apple does not delete the entire server account. Deleting your account in this Game does not delete accounts in the Company’s other games or your Google or Apple account. A new purchase or waiver of refund rights is not a condition of deletion.
