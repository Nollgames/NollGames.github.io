3. Legal basis and public information

Authentication, game saving, rankings, fulfillment and support are processed as necessary to perform the service contract; statutory transaction records are retained to meet legal duties. Security and troubleshooting use an appropriate legal basis and necessary scope. Firebase analytics is processed from the first app launch to the extent necessary to check errors and stability and improve the service; you may request suspension at any time under section 7. Advertising, analytics and transfers requiring separate consent follow that procedure. Publication of this policy or acceptance of the Terms does not replace required separate consent.

Public rankings may show your nickname, selected country, race scores, records, position and display information such as the selected driver. Email addresses, authentication tokens and purchase receipts are not shown in rankings. Avoid using your real name or contact details in a public nickname.

We do not disclose data beyond the described processing without a legal basis. Where separate disclosure requires consent, we identify the recipient, purpose, data and retention. Lawful court or authority requests are handled only within the law.

7. Advertising, analytics and choices

Advertising uses AdMob consent and privacy flows and device settings. Where required for your region or status, “Ad privacy choices” appears in app Settings and opens the available provider controls. Restricting personalized ads or denying tracking does not eliminate all processing for delivery, measurement and security.

Use Android’s available advertising-ID reset or deletion controls or iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. Menu names and options depend on OS and release version.

Firebase analytics operates from the first app launch. To request suspension or withdrawal concerning Firebase or Unity analytics, or deletion of existing analytics or advertising information, contact admin@nollgames.com. We verify the account and scope and explain the result and any lawful restriction. Terms acceptance alone does not justify processing requiring separate consent.

The app stores login, language, sound, Terms choices, progress and unfinished operations on the device. Clearing storage can affect preferences and guest access; it is different from deleting the server account. These policy pages contain no Company-added advertising or analytics scripts or tracking cookies. GitHub separately handles website requests.

8. Access, correction, deletion and objection

You may request access, correction, deletion, suspension and withdrawal of consent, including through an authorized representative, at admin@nollgames.com. After minimum necessary verification, we respond without undue delay within applicable deadlines or explain the legal basis and scope of a restriction.

Use Settings > Delete Account to request withdrawal of your MetaRace v2 account after confirmation. If the app is inaccessible, use the email path on the account and data deletion page. We confirm the scope of deletion of server accounts, progress, rankings and inventory, and additional analytics or advertising provider requests. Statutory transaction and dispute records are retained separately under section 5.

Uninstalling, logging out or unlinking Google or Apple does not delete the entire server account. Deleting MetaRace v2 does not delete other games or your Google or Apple account. A new purchase or waiver of refund rights is not a condition of deletion.
