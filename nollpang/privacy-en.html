3. Grounds for processing and choices

We process account, progress, purchase and support data as necessary to perform the service contract. We retain legally required transaction records to meet legal obligations. Security and fault handling are limited to what is necessary for safe service operation. Where separate consent is required, processing relies on that consent.

Reading this policy or accepting the Terms of Service does not by itself constitute blanket consent to processing that requires separate consent. We provide the required notice and obtain any required consent when purposes change or additional consent is needed.

Linking a Google or Apple account is a choice. Without processing necessary account and game data, we may be unable to provide the corresponding online service. Section 7 describes advertising, analytics and how to request restrictions.

7. Advertising, analytics, local storage and choices

The app uses Google Analytics to analyze events and may provide ads through AdMob and Unity Ads. The current app configuration does not enable ad-personalization permission signals. Identifiers or device data may still be processed for ad delivery, measurement, frequency control and security.

  • Where presented for your region and service configuration, use Google’s ad privacy-choice screen. You can also follow privacy-choice links within ads.
  • On Android, use the advertising settings under Google/privacy in device settings to delete or reset your advertising ID where supported. Menu names vary by OS version.
  • On iOS, manage tracking permission in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. Availability of an advertising ID depends on OS permissions.
  • To withdraw analytics consent, request restriction or ask for deletion of existing analytics/ad data, email admin@nollgames.com. Staff will verify the account and explain the scope and handling of the request.

The app may store sign-in state, language, audio preferences and choice settings on your device. Clearing device storage can affect settings and guest access; it is not the same as deleting server-side account or purchase records.

These legal-information pages contain no advertising or visitor-analytics scripts or tracking cookies added by NollPang. GitHub, the hosting provider, may process IP addresses and other web request data under its own policy.

8. Your rights and account deletion

You can request access, correction, deletion, restriction and withdrawal of consent, including through an authorized representative. Contact admin@nollgames.com; Settings > Customer Support in the app opens the same email contact.

Account deletion is handled by staff after an email request. See account and data deletion instructions. Use the subject “NollPang account deletion request” and send your UID (view/copy in Settings), nickname and reply email if available. You can still contact us if you cannot find your UID.

After checking account ownership and the scope of the request, we act without undue delay within applicable legal time limits and explain the result or reasons for any restriction. Verification is limited to what is necessary; we do not ask for passwords or verification codes.

Deletion covers the service account and linked progress, rankings and owned content. Legally required transaction/dispute records are separated and retained under Section 4. Backups and SDKs may require additional deletion cycles. If a legal exception restricts a right, we explain the basis and reason.

Uninstalling or signing out does not delete the server account. Deleting the NollPang account does not delete your Google or Apple account. Progress and items may not be recoverable after deletion, but we do not make privacy rights conditional on purchases or waiver of refund rights. Statutory refund rights remain protected.

9. Children and security

The current service is not intended for children under 14. If we learn that a child’s data has been processed without the required legal basis, we take appropriate verification, deletion or other protective action. Parents/guardians can contact support. Higher local age or parental-consent requirements continue to apply where relevant.

We apply safeguards such as encrypted service connections, authenticated access controls and restricted access to personal data. You may ask staff to review concerns about automated game rewards, rankings or abuse checks.
